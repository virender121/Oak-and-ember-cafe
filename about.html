Our story

Made with heart,
served with warmth.

We created Oak & Ember to be the easy choice for a great coffee, honest food and a comfortable place to spend time.

How it started

A simple idea: make the local café feel special again.

Our story begins with the belief that a café can be more than a quick stop. It can be a familiar corner of the neighborhood — somewhere to meet a friend, open a laptop, bring the family, or enjoy ten quiet minutes with a warm cup in your hands.

That idea shapes everything we do, from our relaxed room and approachable menu to the way we welcome every guest.
